How does real-time AI web search work?

When you ask about recent events, weather, or other time-sensitive topics, AskWise can look up live sources and show reference links alongside its answer. Availability depends on internet access and AI service capabilities.

Where are my chats, recordings, and notes stored?

Chats, recordings, notes, and tasks are stored in private app storage on your Android device. When you request AI answers or transcription, the content necessary to process that request is sent to OpenAI. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I record meetings with my screen locked?

Yes. Start a recording while AskWise is visible; an active microphone foreground service may continue recording if you lock the screen or switch apps, subject to Android permissions and device restrictions. Make sure you have permission to record everyone involved.

How do I restore AskWise Pro on a new phone?

Sign in to Google Play with the same Google account used for the subscription. In AskWise, open Settings and select Restore Purchases. If it still does not appear, contact support with your purchase order ID (not your payment card details).

How can I contact AskWise support?

Visit the Help & Support page or email chatpro@techtornix.com. We will help with app setup, subscriptions, recording, and privacy requests.
