Getting started

3 helpful answers

How do I get started with AskWise?

Download AskWise from Google Play, open the app, and choose AI Chat, Meeting Recorder, Notes, or Agenda. No separate AskWise account is required for the app’s local content features.

Can I use AskWise without an internet connection?

Your locally saved notes, tasks and recordings may remain available offline. AI chat, live web search, transcription and some purchase operations require an internet connection.

How does real-time AI web search work?

AskWise can search live web sources for timely questions and provide reference links with an answer. AI results should still be checked against the original sources.

Recording meetings

4 helpful answers

How do I record a meeting?

Open Meeting Recorder in AskWise, grant microphone permission if prompted, and tap Record while the app is visible. You can stop recording from the app or the active recording controls.

Can a meeting keep recording when I lock my phone?

An already-started foreground microphone recording can normally continue when the screen locks or you switch apps, subject to Android and manufacturer restrictions. Start it while the app is open and follow the local recording-consent rules.

Where are my meeting recordings and summaries stored?

Original recordings and saved notes are stored in private app storage. Content selected for AI transcription or summaries is transmitted to OpenAI to provide that feature. Review our Privacy Policy for provider retention details.

Can I export or remove recordings?

Use the available recording options to delete recordings and, if your installed app version supports it, share or export the output. If an export action is unavailable, contact support with your app version.

Purchases & Pro

3 helpful answers

How do I restore my AskWise Pro subscription?

Use the same Google account in Play Store that originally purchased AskWise Pro. Open AskWise Settings → Restore Purchases. If it does not appear, restart the app and contact support with your Google Play order ID.

How can I cancel a subscription?

Open Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → AskWise → Cancel subscription. Uninstalling the app alone does not cancel billing.

How do refunds work?

Google Play handles app billing. Check Google Play’s refund policy, or email support with the order ID and a short description of your issue. Never send your bank card number.

Reminders & widgets

3 helpful answers

How do I create a reminder?

Open Agenda, add a task, choose the available reminder date or time, and allow notification permission when requested. Reminders are created locally on the device.

Why am I not receiving notifications?

Check AskWise notification permissions, battery optimization settings, do-not-disturb mode, and whether the task has a reminder time scheduled.

How do widgets work?

Touch and hold your Android home screen, select Widgets, find AskWise, and add the available Agenda, Notes, or other app widget to your home screen.

Privacy & data

3 helpful answers

Is my chat history stored in the cloud?

AskWise keeps its app-managed conversation history in private local storage instead of an AskWise cloud account. When you ask AI to process a prompt or audio, the relevant data is sent to OpenAI for that request.

How do I remove my AskWise data?

Delete chats, notes, tasks and recordings from the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling also removes most local content. Android backup copies, if any, follow your device’s settings and provider policies.

How can I request privacy assistance?

Email chatpro@techtornix.com with the subject Privacy Request. We can help with information we control, such as support communications.
