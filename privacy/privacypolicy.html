01. Introduction

AskWise: AI Chat Assistant (package name com.chatpro.assistant) is an Android productivity application offered under the TechTornix Solutions brand (“AskWise,” “we,” “our,” or “us”). This policy describes what information the app processes, where it is stored, who receives it, and the choices available to you.

Our approach is local-first: chats, smart notes, tasks, and original voice recordings are saved in the app’s private storage on your device. Some functions, however, require transmission of the information you choose to process to an external AI provider. Local-first does not mean that every AI request stays entirely on your phone.

02. Information we process

Chats & searches

Questions, prompts and web-search requests you submit, plus the AI responses returned to you.

Audio & transcripts

Recordings you create and audio you choose to transcribe, along with resulting summaries.

Productivity content

Notes, pinned notes, tasks, reminders and widget content stored locally on your device.

Service information

Limited configuration, network connectivity and billing entitlement data required by app services.

AskWise does not require a separate AskWise account to use its local content features. If you email support, we receive the contact information and message you provide so we can reply.

03. Android permissions & purpose

Permissions are used to enable specific features. Availability and the exact permission prompts can vary by Android version and device.

RECORD_AUDIOMicrophone access

Captures voice notes or meeting audio only when you intentionally start recording and grant permission. The app may keep an already-started recording active after you lock your screen.

FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONERecording service

Supports an ongoing microphone foreground service with an active notification when recording continues outside the visible app, subject to Android restrictions.

POST_NOTIFICATIONSNotifications

Displays agenda/reminder notifications and, where required, recording service alerts.

INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEConnectivity

Enables requests to AI services, downloads configuration data and checks online connectivity.

RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDRestoring reminders

Allows eligible scheduled reminders to be rescheduled after a device restart; it is not used to automatically start microphone recording.

Important: Android restricts starting microphone foreground services from the background. Begin a recording while AskWise is open. Recording other people may require their consent under local law.

04. Storage, security & local data

Chat history, notes and agenda items are stored in app-private SQLite databases. Original audio files are saved in the app’s private file storage. The Android operating system provides application sandboxing, but no storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

We do not operate an AskWise cloud account service or maintain your complete conversation history as a synchronized account database. Your device may nevertheless participate in Android or manufacturer backup systems, depending on your backup settings and the app’s configured backup behavior. Uninstalling normally removes the app’s local data, but existing device or cloud backups may be governed by your backup provider’s rules.

Deleting an item in AskWise removes the associated local copy through the application’s controls. Deletion of information already sent to an external processor is governed by that processor’s retention policies.

05. Third-party processors

OpenAI APIAI answers • web/search features • speech transcription

When you use an AI feature, selected prompts, relevant conversation context, search requests, or audio files required for transcription are sent to OpenAI for processing. According to OpenAI’s published API policy, API data is not used to train its models by default unless an account explicitly opts in. OpenAI may retain abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days by default, with exceptions for legal and safety reasons and certain API features. See OpenAI’s data controls documentation.

Google Firebase Realtime Database (Google LLC)Dynamic configuration

Used to retrieve app configuration, such as AI connectivity settings. Configuration requests may expose ordinary network metadata (such as an IP address) to the provider. This integration is not used as an AskWise cloud backup for your private conversations.

Google Play Billing (Google LLC)Subscriptions and purchase validation

Google Play processes payments, subscription management, and entitlement information. AskWise does not collect or store payment-card numbers. Google may process your purchase identifiers and account information under its own terms.

AskWise does not include third-party advertising SDKs or sell your personal information to data brokers, based on the app configuration described in this policy.

06. Data retention & deletion

  • Local app content: Remains on your device until you delete the item, clear app data, or uninstall AskWise, subject to device backup behavior.
  • AI requests: Processed by OpenAI to deliver the requested feature; OpenAI’s applicable API retention policies and exceptions determine the duration of any provider-side logs or application state.
  • Subscription records: Managed by Google Play according to Google’s policies and relevant legal obligations.
  • Support emails: Retained only as needed to respond, resolve issues and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations.

To delete your local content, use the app’s available delete controls. You can also use Android Settings to clear the app’s data or uninstall the app. For questions about support-related information, email chatpro@techtornix.com.

07. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the European Union’s GDPR and California’s CCPA/CPRA to request access, correction, deletion, portability, or restriction of certain personal information, or to object to certain processing. Not every right applies to every type of data or circumstance.

Because most AskWise content exists only on your device, the app’s local controls are generally the fastest way to review or remove it. For information we actually hold or control, such as support messages, send a request to chatpro@techtornix.com. We may need to verify the request and will respond as required by applicable law.

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are commonly used in the CCPA/CPRA. We do not knowingly discriminate against users for exercising applicable privacy rights.

08. International transfers & security

External processors such as OpenAI and Google may process information in countries outside your own. Their policies explain applicable security and cross-border processing arrangements. Please avoid including passwords, payment-card details, or highly sensitive personal information in AI requests unless necessary.

We use the Android app sandbox and provider connections designed to protect information in transit, but no networked service is free of risk. Keep your Android device and AskWise updated and protect your device with a secure lock screen.

09. Children’s privacy

AskWise is not intentionally directed at children under 13 or any higher minimum age required by applicable local law. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children through AskWise. If you believe a child has submitted information to us through support, contact us so that we can assess and address the concern.

10. Policy changes & contact

We may revise this policy to reflect changes in the app, service providers, or legal obligations. The effective date at the top will be updated when a revised policy takes effect. We recommend periodically reviewing this page.
